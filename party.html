We’ve been sitting here trying to figure out how to start this letter. It’s hard, because we think there’s a lot to say. The short version is this: we’re genuinely thrilled to be sharing such an important day with all of you. Picturing everyone standing beside us as we say our vows just fills us with glee.

The longer version is that every one of you have had a profound impact on our lives—in ways obviously big to subtly small—and we're just so fortunate to have you in our lives. Our ideas of what courage, family, community and love look like have been deeply influenced by your wellspring of generosity and kindness. If ever you’ve helped us move house (something we've done a lot!); if you’ve ever been a listening ear while we’ve ranted about something unjust; if you’ve ever offered personal advice, career coaching, portfolio-finessing or talked us down from any hypothetical ledges; or even if you’ve just picked us up from the train station, watered our houseplants or fed our cat. We’d be nothing without all of the parts of ourselves that’ve been dutifully assembled with your help.

Our wedding weekend is just as much for y'all as it is for us. Heritage Barn offers two gorgeous on-site houses for the party to stay in, which you’ll learn more about below. We invite you to please make yourselves at home with us, and we hope you’re as stoked as we are to celebrate our love together. See you soon!

— Megan & Joey
