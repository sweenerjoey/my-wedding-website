We’ve been sitting here trying to figure out how to start this letter. It’s hard, because we think there’s a lot to say. The short version is this: we’re genuinely thrilled to be sharing such an important day for us with all of you. Picturing everyone standing beside us as we say our vows fills us with glee.

The longer version is that every one of you have had a deep and profound impact on our lives—in ways obviously big to achingly small—and we feel so fortunate that you're in our lives. What courage, family, community and love looks like have been deeply influenced by your generosity and kindness, whether we’ve said that out loud or not. If ever you’ve helped us move house; if you’ve ever been a listening ear while we’ve ranted; if you’ve ever offered personal or professional advice, career coaching, portfolio-finessing or talked us down from our perceived ledges; or even if you’ve just picked us up from the train station, watered our houseplants or feed our cat. We’d be nothing without all of the parts of ourselves that’ve been dutifully assembled by your helping hands.

Our wedding weekend is just as much for y'all as it is for us. Heritage Barn offers two gorgeous on-site houses for the party to stay in, which you’ll learn more about below. We invite you to please make yourselves at home with us, and we hope you’re as stoked as we are to celebrate our love together. See you soon!

— Megan & Joey
