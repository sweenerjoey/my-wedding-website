We’ve been trying to figure out how to write this letter, because we have a lot to say. The short version is: we’re honored to be sharing such an important day with each and every one of you. We're filled with glee imagining everyone standing beside next to us!

The longer version is that you've all have had such a deep, profound impact on our lives—in ways obviously big and subtly small—that we feel so unbelievably fortunate to have you in our lives. Our understanding of what family, community and love look like have been shaped by your wellspring of generosity and kindness. If ever you’ve helped us move house (something we've done a lot!); if you’ve ever been a listening ear while we’ve ranted for too long about something we can't seem to let go of; if you’ve ever offered personal advice, professional advice, portfolio-finessing or talked us down from any hypothetical ledges; or even if you’ve just picked us up from the train station, watered our houseplants or fed our cat. We’d be nothing without all of the parts of ourselves that’ve been dutifully assembled with your help.

Our wedding weekend is just as much for y'all as it is for us. Heritage Barn offers two gorgeous on-site houses for the wedding party to stay in, which you can learn more about below. We invite you to please make yourselves at home with us, and we hope you’re as stoked as we are to celebrate our love together. See you soon!

— Megan & Joey

The Fine Print

Who’s Invited?

Because of the first-come, first-serve nature of Heritage Barn’s onsite lodging—and because both sets of parents are booked to stay on-site, too—only members of the wedding party are invited to stay with us, not your families. Thanks for understanding.

When Can we Get There?

We have access to the property for the whole weekend; we can arrive as early as 10:00 A.M. on Friday, Aug. 27th, and are free to stay until 12:00 P.M. on Sunday. You don’t have to stay the entire time, especially if your family is lodging elsewhere, but we'd encourage you to so that we can do things like barbecues, drinks and brunches!

Please Be respectful.

The accommodations are included in the cost of the venue, and we’re happy to have you all joining us. Though we’ve also purchased event insurance, we kindly ask that you be respectful of the property and the houses while you’re with us.

We don’t wanna have to pay extra if something gets damaged. #sorrynotsorry

No after-Parties.

We have explicit instructions from the venue owners that the houses cannot be used for after-parties. We would instead strongly encourage you to explore downtown Hamilton and enjoy a bit of bar-hopping (we might just join ya!).
